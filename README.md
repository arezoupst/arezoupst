### about

<div align="center">

**BSc Computer Science** · **MSc Bioinformatics**  
Tehran Polytechnic (Amirkabir University of Technology)

</div>

<br/>

I am a bioinformatics researcher working where **biology**, **machine learning**, and **healthcare** overlap. I came from computer science and moved into bioinformatics to work with biological data that is incomplete, noisy, and tied to real patients.

At the Computational Biology Research Center I study **pregnancy and postpartum multi-omics**. That includes biomarker-related questions, gestational age, and models that have to hold up on a person they have not seen — which is why I use **leave-one-subject-out** evaluation, including Siamese networks. The dataset is private.

Alongside research, I am an R&D assistant at [Learnify Health](https://www.linkedin.com/company/learnifyhealth), working on virtual-patient scenarios for medical education. I was also a teaching assistant for Introduction to Bioinformatics at Sharif University of Technology.

I am still learning, and I am building toward ML / AI engineering: clearer code, careful evaluation, and work I can explain.
